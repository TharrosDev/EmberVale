## Phase 50 — Side Content, Activities & World Density [C]

> Phase 44's empty-space budget is a constraint, not a backlog. Content is distributed by travel
> rhythm and realm identity, never by filling every cell. Lairs/dungeons use rooms, enemies, doors,
> loot and bosses already shipped—no puzzle, trap or vault framework.

### Approved content categories and targets

Before authoring, create a per-realm sheet for side quests, emergent encounters, lairs, exploration
rewards, environmental stories, guild/bounty/companion content, collectibles and ambient behaviors.
Targets are ranges derived from realm size, playtime and measured discovery spacing, not arbitrary
quotas. Each row names travel-time spacing, critical-path visibility, repeatability, map-pin policy,
reward family, owner and protected “leave empty” cells.

- [ ] **50A — Distribution matrix and discovery-density baseline** [C/P]
  - **Build / Author:** walk/capture every realm route; measure location/encounter gaps; set provisional
    category ranges and protect transitional/dead-end cells. Quality bar: premise, escalation/choice,
    consequence, reward, failure/recovery and map/flag ownership.
  - **Verify:** no quota requires protected cells; every category has a system owner.
  - **Done when:** the matrix can accept/reject a pitch by identity, pacing and budget.

- [ ] **50B — Ember Crown side/exploration batch** [C]
  - **Build / Author:** a reviewable batch spanning civilization, trade and fading hope; mix NPC quest,
    lair/encounter, exploration reward and environmental story; reuse strong existing locations.
  - **Verify:** critical overlap, early target, overflow, map policy and spacing.
  - **Done when:** targets are met without adding beats to protected empty cells.

- [ ] **50C — Frostfang side/wilderness batch** [C]
  - **Build / Author:** clan/dragon/alpine expedition fiction, remote lair/hunt, vista/story rewards and
    sparse ambient life; preserve long traverses. No survival needs.
  - **Verify:** factions/dragons, vertical routes, weather, mount, recovery and spacing.
  - **Done when:** content rhythm remains sparse and expeditionary.

- [ ] **50D — Ashen Wilds side/anomaly batch** [C]
  - **Build / Author:** survivor choices, ruins, corrupted ecology and existing-system anomaly encounters;
    one lair and environmental chains without new mechanics.
  - **Verify:** combat-build coverage, event filters, recovery, early-cleared sites and map policy.
  - **Done when:** danger/ruin identity is strong and wilderness remains dominant.

- [ ] **50E — Sunspire side/civilization batch** [C]
  - **Build / Author:** library/cult/civil authority, caravan/jungle and buried-history content; consume
    cult outcomes without requiring infiltration.
  - **Verify:** cult matrix, water/routes, schedules, localization and discovery.
  - **Done when:** scholarship, trade and religious control have distinct content.

- [ ] **50F — Pale Concord side/preservation batch** [C]
  - **Build / Author:** release/preservation dilemmas, frozen routines, environmental stories and one
    appropriate lair, available only after discovery.
  - **Verify:** secrecy, 44N rules, state variants, save/load and no ordinary-undead reskin.
  - **Done when:** content deepens the bargain and hidden-realm contract.

- [ ] **50G — Lair/dungeon and exploration-reward pass** [C]
  - **Build / Author:** review entrances, encounter escalation, exit/backtracking, champion/boss, loot
    ownership and save points using architecture, doors and combat.
  - **Do not:** add puzzle locks, traps, vault trials or cut frameworks.
  - **Verify:** enter/exit after clear, pre-killed target, save/reload, full pack and map.
  - **Done when:** every lair has a complete combat/exploration loop.

- [ ] **50H — World events and wilderness pacing** [C]
  - **Build / Author:** region/day/weather/story-filtered tables, pressure caps, quiet travel windows,
    repeat cooldown and duplicate-reward policy.
  - **Verify:** statistical distribution, filters, worst overlap and save/re-roll policy.
  - **Done when:** routes feel alive without continuous busyness.

- [ ] **50I — Guild bounties, companion quests and collectibles** [C]
  - **Build / Author:** Phase 42 follow-ups; Nyra/Orik/Seraphine/Vex loyalty quests with absence fallbacks;
    lore books and exploration sets feeding 50.5.
  - **Verify:** guild refused/left, companion unrecruited/full party/loyalty, duplicate pickup and overflow.
  - **Done when:** every named guild/companion has bounded optional content without story gates.

- [ ] **50J — Ambient behavior and realm acceptance pass** [C/P]
  - **Build / Author:** schedules/dialogue/ambient actions by settlement role; compare actual counts,
    spacing/playtime to targets; merge/remove weak density; audit inaccessible content, flags and empty land.
  - **Verify:** day/night/state routes, all pins, performance and full-realm traversals.
  - **Done when:** targets are evidenced, all content reachable and quiet land remains intentional.
