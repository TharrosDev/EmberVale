## Phase 53 — Art Complete & World Beautification [P]

> Follow ART_STYLE, ASSET_POLICY and WORLD_AUTHORING. Quaternius is the standing 3D set; terrain stays
> semantic/no-photo-texture; geography stays in landforms. Art may detail Phase 44 but may not move stable
> ids, routes, map anchors or completed Ember Crown/Frostfang geography without migration and full QA.

- [ ] **53A — Art coverage/placeholder census and realm visual bibles** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** matrix environment kit, POIs/interiors, characters/creatures/bosses, weapons/items,
    animation, VFX, lighting/world states and cinematics; classify final/acceptable/missing/placeholder.
    Approve one-page identity per realm and asset/licence/performance budgets.
  - **Done when:** every visible asset has an owner and “art complete” is measurable.

- [ ] **53B — Ember Crown environment/lighting acceptance** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** final materials, dressing, interiors and state variants without revisiting closed
    geography; preserve six transitional cells and dying-heartland identity.
  - **Verify:** route/POI shot set, map/traversal/world QA, day/weather/states and perf delta.
  - **Done when:** no unapproved greybox/placeholder remains in the realm.

- [ ] **53C — Frostfang environment/lighting acceptance** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** alpine materials/silhouettes, settlements/lairs/interiors and states while preserving
    cold-light and five empty traverses.
  - **Verify:** same gates plus snow/rock readability and long-view HLOD.
  - **Done when:** no placeholder and realm remains unmistakably alpine.

- [ ] **53D — Ashen Wilds environment/lighting acceptance** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** Cataclysm terrain detail, corrupted forest/ruins/settlements/lairs and world states;
    props reinforce, never create landforms.
  - **Verify:** route shots, repetition, combat readability, traversal and performance.
  - **Done when:** final identity covers every critical/side route without over-dressing wilderness.

- [ ] **53E — Sunspire environment/lighting acceptance** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** desert/jungle/library/cult architecture, water/shore and state variants; distinct ancient
    civilization/cult layers.
  - **Verify:** glare/contrast, routes/water, interiors, HLOD and worst hub performance.
  - **Done when:** no placeholder and climate/culture remain readable.

- [ ] **53F — Pale Concord environment/lighting acceptance** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** stalled dusk, preserved settlements/fields, hidden entry, Hollow territory and ending states;
    avoid ordinary haunted-field shorthand.
  - **Verify:** hidden/revealed captures, readable dusk combat/UI, state variants and performance.
  - **Done when:** preservation premise reads in every major space.

- [ ] **53G — Characters, companions, guilds and cult models** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** unique silhouette/wardrobe/color/material coverage, first/third-person arms, equipment
    attachment/clipping and cinematic LOD; reuse rigs/retarget library.
  - **Verify:** animation set, gear combinations, cameras, cinematics, color-independent faction recognition.
  - **Done when:** no named critical actor uses an unapproved generic stand-in.

- [ ] **53H — Creature, dragon and boss final assets/animation** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** roster silhouette/rig/material, attack telegraph alignment, hit zones, phase/vision assets
    and LOD/HLOD; never let art move damage windows invisibly.
  - **Verify:** every attack/phase/body zone, camera distances, pooling, bestiary and performance.
  - **Done when:** all combatants are final and tells remain honest.

- [ ] **53I — Weapon/item/VFX/cinematic polish** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** catalogue models/icons/category fallback audit, melee/ranged/spell/impact/corruption/
    world-state VFX and final sequence assets; reduced-motion/readability alternatives.
  - **Verify:** inventory/world drops/first-person, every spell school/rarity, worst effect stack and skip cleanup.
  - **Done when:** all approved content has final readable presentation.

- [ ] **53J — Global visual cohesion and art-complete gate** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** full placeholder search, lighting/material/scale/repetition/LOD/readability pass and asset
    manifest; compare visual baselines only after human inspection.
  - **Verify:** all world/panel/HUD/cinematic captures, traversal, mesh census and perf on worst scenes.
  - **Done when:** coverage matrix is 100% final/explicitly accepted, zero unapproved greybox and no gate regression.
