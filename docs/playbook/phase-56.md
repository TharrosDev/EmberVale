## Phase 56 — Balance & Difficulty Tuning [C/P]

> Change data/resources through measured hypotheses. Every pass records build/save fixture, scenario,
> sample size, before/after distributions and player-facing conclusion. Do not add durability, needs,
> encumbrance or new mechanics as an economy/balance fix.

- [ ] **56A — Instrumentation, cohorts and target bands** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** define representative fresh/average/optimized melee/magic/ranged/stealth builds at
    early/mid/end progression; encounter/boss/economy fixtures; analytics events/reports for TTK, damage taken,
    deaths, resource use, XP/gold/loot/corruption. Set provisional bands from playtests, label assumptions.
  - **Done when:** every later tuning claim can be reproduced from a fixture/report.

- [ ] **56B — Player combat/build balance** [C/P]
  - **Measure/Tune:** weapon families, spells/schools, ranged draw, stamina/mana/poise, armor/resists/crit/status,
    consumables and affix/set synergies; protect “read over mash” and no immunity/class trap.
  - **Verify:** cohort×representative enemy matrix, outliers and regression of feedback/tells.
  - **Done when:** every build spine falls inside approved performance bands with distinct tradeoffs.

- [ ] **56C — Enemy/encounter/boss difficulty** [C/P]
  - **Measure/Tune:** enemy role pressure, group composition, event overlap, bosses by phase, adds, telegraphs,
    retry distance and companion impact across difficulties.
  - **Verify:** solo/companion, each cohort/difficulty, no unavoidable/unreadable hit and no trivial dominant answer.
  - **Done when:** completion/death/TTK bands hold and difficulty comes from reads, not health inflation alone.

- [ ] **56D — XP, level and perk progression** [C/P]
  - **Measure/Tune:** XP sources, quest/combat split, level timing, skill points, cap reach and realm-order variance;
    prevent grind requirements and overleveling one route into irrelevance.
  - **Verify:** modeled and real critical/side-content routes, no-farm/farm extremes and max-level behavior.
  - **Done when:** progression produces regular build choices across all valid story orders.

- [ ] **56E — Economy, crafting and gold sinks** [C/P]
  - **Measure/Tune:** income/source, prices/spreads/demand, services/travel/property/mercenary/commission sinks,
    recipes/materials and inventory opportunity; use --economy and negative battery.
  - **Do not:** introduce wear/repair or needs.
  - **Verify:** poor/typical/trader/completionist routes, exploits, same-counter round trip and required purchases.
  - **Done when:** scarcity is meaningful, trade can pay by travel, and no mandatory path requires grind.

- [ ] **56F — Loot/reward and corruption pacing** [C/P]
  - **Measure/Tune:** rarity/affix/source frequency, duplicate pressure, unique timing, upgrades per session;
    power-versus-corruption offers, tier timing and legitimate reachability of both endings.
  - **Verify:** seeded roll distributions, full inventory, realm orders, accept/refuse extremes and threshold boundaries.
  - **Done when:** loot regularly poses choices and both corruption paths are attractive/reachable.

- [ ] **56G — Difficulty modes, assists and balance sign-off** [C/P]
  - **Measure/Tune:** allowed damage/aggression/timing/aim options; preserve story/reward access and telegraph honesty.
    Rerun all cohorts, economy/XP/corruption reports and worst boss scenarios; publish final values/rationale.
  - **Done when:** modes match descriptions, no mode invalidates a build, no critical outlier remains and evidence is signed.
