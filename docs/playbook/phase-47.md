## Phase 47 — Main Story, Act II: Gathering the Flame `[C]`

> Each realm arc is two sessions: **entry/alliance/investigation** then **boss/relic/consequence**.
> Arcs may be completed in the approved order model recorded below; no arc assumes optional guild,
> cult or companion content. Every resident boss uses Phase 36 and every defeat uses Phase 43.5/44.5.

- [ ] **47A — Act II order model and shared relic state graph** `[C/P]`
  - **Build / Author:** decide open/semi-ordered realm access; prerequisite graph, relic accepted/refused/
    claimed states, corruption transaction, alliance inputs, Ashen Knight seeds and fallback entries.
    Define unique-reward overflow/duplicate policy and Act III convergence.
  - **Verify:** graph reachability/cycle tests and every realm-first/last permutation allowed.
  - **Done when:** no realm arc or relic can orphan another and six power outcomes are representable.

- [ ] **47B — Ember Crown investigation and alliance beat** `[C]`
  - **Build / Author:** Iron King rule consequences, Dawnwarden/Syndicate/Emberbound optional hooks,
    civilian stakes, route/map/dialogue/cutscene and boss-access fallback.
  - **Verify:** guild combinations, boss killed early, high corruption, Kael states, save at access.
  - **Done when:** every path reaches the arena for a legible reason.

- [ ] **47C — Iron King confrontation, relic and realm consequence** `[C]`
  - **Build / Author:** final pre-boss choice, Phase 36 fight, vision, relic/corruption choice, world-state
    transition, rewards and alliance/epilogue flags.
  - **Verify:** defeat/choice/vision skip/reload, full pack, pre-defeated boss and post-state return.
  - **Done when:** all outcomes converge to an explicit next lead without duplicating rewards.

- [ ] **47D — Frostfang investigation and clan alliance beat** `[C]`
  - **Build / Author:** Storm Tyrant history/route, Frostfang Clans/Ash Hunters/dragon optional inputs,
    vertical travel, settlement dialogue and boss access.
  - **Verify:** clan betrayal/ranks, Ancient killed/spared, weather/mount, boss early and no guild.
  - **Done when:** culturally distinct alliance paths reach the Tyrant.

- [ ] **47E — Storm Tyrant confrontation and consequence** `[C]`
  - **Build / Author:** storm territory set-piece, boss/vision/relic/corruption, clan/dragon and realm-state
    aftermath; reward/map cleanup.
  - **Verify:** combat-build coverage, reload across weather/cinematic, branch/world state.
  - **Done when:** Frostfang state and carried flags are complete.

- [ ] **47F — Ashen Wilds investigation and survivor alliance** `[C]`

- [ ] **47G — Beast Lord confrontation and consequence** `[C]`
