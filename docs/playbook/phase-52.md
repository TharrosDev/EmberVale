## Phase 52 — Full Audio & Music Production [P]

> Reuse AudioDirector/MusicDirector/AmbienceDirector and canonical buses/cue ids. Unknown cues currently
> synthesize placeholders; “audio complete” requires an audited coverage sheet, not silence-free playback.
> VO languages/scope are unresolved and must pass 52A before recording.

- [ ] **52A — Audio coverage census, VO scope and asset/licence gate** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** matrix every realm/state/weather/time, combat verb/surface, spell, creature/boss,
    UI, cinematic and dialogue tier; identify procedural/real/missing cues. Decide voiced scope, casting,
    recording language(s), subtitle parity and whether non-key dialogue remains text-only; licence/naming budget.
  - **Verify:** every live cue caller maps to a row; no platform/language promise invented.
  - **Done when:** signed scope, source/licence policy and production priority exist.

- [ ] **52B — Realm music identities and adaptive state beds** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** explore/safe/combat variants or approved stems per realm, transitions through existing
    state machine, loop points/loudness metadata and world-state variants where budgeted.
  - **Verify:** state priority/crossfade, region travel, pause/load, long loops and bus sliders.
  - **Done when:** five realms are distinguishable and transitions never click/restart incorrectly.

- [ ] **52C — Boss, rival and ending music** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** themes/cues for six fallen, Morthul, rival duels and both endings; reuse motifs without
    forcing one full track per scene; cinematic/music handoff and skip cleanup.
  - **Verify:** intro→combat→phase→defeat/escape→vision, reload/skip and no overlapping beds.
  - **Done when:** every tentpole has a complete state path and no procedural fallback.

- [ ] **52D — Realm/weather/time ambience** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** beds/spot emitters per biome, settlement, water, weather and day phase; sparse wilderness
    remains sparse; positional range/voice limits and surface metadata audit.
  - **Verify:** every realm/weather/time combination, transitions, listener movement, pool/CPU and bus controls.
  - **Done when:** ambience coverage matrix has no unapproved placeholder or dead zone.

- [ ] **52E — Combat, weapons, impacts and footsteps** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** melee/ranged/block/parry/dodge/poise, material impacts and all terrain/prop surfaces;
    distinct player/enemy/dragon weight; avoid masking telegraphs.
  - **Verify:** rapid overlap/voice stealing, every surface, camera modes, mounted combat and mix readability.
  - **Done when:** every combat state speaks distinctly and cues remain legible in worst fights.

- [ ] **52F — Spells, creatures and world-interaction SFX** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** cast/loop/release/impact/status by spell school/delivery, creature locomotion/vocal/
    attacks/death, doors/loot/economy/housing/mount/water recovery.
  - **Verify:** school matrix, channel cancel, pooled projectiles, creature roster and unknown-cue report.
  - **Done when:** coverage sheet contains no unapproved synthetic fallback.

- [ ] **52G — UI, cinematics and dialogue/VO integration** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** semantic UI cues, cinematic cue assets, approved VO lines with takes/metadata/Loc key
    mapping, subtitle timing and fallback to text; Voice bus and ducking/mix policy.
  - **Verify:** controller UI storm, skip/locale/subtitles, missing VO, dialogue choice and accessibility.
  - **Done when:** approved scope is fully integrated and text-only content remains intentionally scoped.

- [ ] **52H — Mix, loudness, placeholder and rights acceptance** [P]
  - **Build / Author:** representative mix scenes, bus headroom/dynamics, cue census proving no procedural
    fallback in shipped scope, asset manifest/licences and hearing-fatigue review.
  - **Verify:** hub/wilderness/boss/cinematic/UI worst cases, volume extremes, mono/stereo device and long play.
  - **Done when:** matrix complete, rights recorded, no clipped/inaudible critical cue and zero unapproved placeholder.
