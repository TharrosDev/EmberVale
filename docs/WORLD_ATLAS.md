# World atlas

> **Data authority:** `tools/world_atlas.py` (bands, crossings, reserved hooks, distance bands) and the
> region specs `tools/region_spec_<realm>.py`. `python tools/world_atlas.py --check` is the gate; the
> table below is generated by `--table` and the gate fails if this page drifts from it.

This page is the 2026-09 world rebuild's contract, and Phase 44A's atlas. It says where every realm
is, how the realms connect, how far places are from each other and why, and how the built realms are
laid out: **geography first, civilisation second, routes third, places fourth, detail last.**

## 3. The Ember Crown

**Geography.** The Crown Range walls the north with one saddle, the Crown Pass. The Emberwash comes off
the range beside the Kingsway, runs down its valley past the capital's west side under the broken
Kingsbridge and into the Tarn; the fens and Hollowreach spread below the lake. The eastern hills hold
Emberdeep; the burnt frontier heath runs under the range to the Emberspire massif and the arena. The
farm belt and the ash flats run south to the border hills, gapped at the Southmarch Gate; the eastern
border hills are gapped at the Ashen Breach.

**Places and why they are there.**

| Place | Why here |
| --- | --- |
| The capital (Crown Square, Embermarket, Bridgeward, Eastgate) | where the realm's roads meet, on the spur the Iron Citadel crowns, beside the river crossing |
| The Iron Citadel (`location.ember_crown.iron_citadel`, Primary landmark) | a 55 x 35 x 51 m fortress (the rts pack's castle, adopted at real size) on the spur's levelled crown, gate to the capital, garrison forecourt at the ramp head; seen from the west road, the Downs, the Kingsway and the Crossway |
| The Crossway Post | the only pass through the range |
| Tarn's Landing | fish, and the lake shore where the river comes in |
| Hollowreach | the drowned old town in the lake's outflow fens |
| Emberdeep | ore in the eastern hills |
| Ashfall Homestead | the player's holding out on the ash flats |
| The Northern Wilds and the Deadfall Lodge | forested upland off the Kingsway |
| The Western Wilds (bandit corrie) | the western ridge above the lake |
| The Ember Arena | the far north-east under the Emberspire: a boss at the end of a journey |

**Route hierarchy.** Primary: the Kingsway (capital → watch ridge → Crossway → pass), the west road
(capital → Kingsbridge ford → Tarn's Landing), the east road (capital → Eastgate → Emberdeep), the
market lane, the Hollowreach road, the farm lane, the caravan road south, and the long east road
(Emberdeep → uplands → arena). Secondary: the frontier road (Crossway → waste → arena, the short
dangerous way), the lakeshore road (Tarn's Landing → Hollowreach), the wilds trail and the Crossway's
west track (a loop through the Northern Wilds), the shore trail to the corrie, the watch-ridge spur and
the citadel ramp. Tertiary: the drovers' track, the fen causeway, the burnt track to the Breach.

**Loops.** Capital → Crossway → frontier road → arena → east road → Emberdeep → capital. Kingsway →
wilds trail → Northern Wilds → Crossway west track → Crossway. Capital → west road → Tarn's Landing →
lakeshore road → Hollowreach → Hollowreach road → Embermarket.

**Landmark chains.** Everywhere: the Iron Citadel over the capital. North: the north gate towers → the ruined watch tower on the ridge → the Crossway
palisade between its spurs → the pass marker stones → the range. West: Bridgeward's gate towers → the
broken Kingsbridge piers → the lake opening → Tarn's Landing. East: Eastgate's towers → the Crown
Stones on the heath to the north → Emberdeep's hill → the Emberspire. South: the knoll farm → the
caravan road across the farm belt → the Southmarch Gate towers. Across the fens: the drowned towers
of old Hollowreach.

**Empty country.** Thirty-six of fifty-two cells carry ground, weather, ecology and at most a road.

## 4. Frostfang Reach

**Geography.** The road enters from the south through a gap in the fells into the Stormbound Vale and
climbs to the Clan Hold on its bench under the mountain wall. The roost ridge closes the west, with one
col; beyond it the Wild dragon's side valley and the Splintered Shelf. The eastern ridge closes the
east, with the burnt col; beyond it the snow turns to ash and the Ash roost's crater. North of the
hold, Glacier Pass climbs between the valley walls to the snow saddle and the Ancient's aerie against
the northern wall. Stormcrown's spire stands in the north-east high passes.

**Culture as traces, not buildings.** Stonewatch (the clan waystation on the vale road), cairns where
roads turn, the abandoned hunting lodge on the western fell, the broken Ravenspur watch, territorial
markers where the dragons' country begins, an old camp under Glacier Pass, broken stones at
Stormcrown's foot. Twenty-seven of thirty-six cells are empty high country.

**Routes.** Primary: the vale road, the glacier road to the aerie. Secondary: the west road over the
roost col, the east road through the burnt col. Loop: roost road → hunters' ridge route → aerie →
glacier road → hold. Tertiary: the hunters' path to the lodge, the Ravenspur spur, the Stormcrown track.

## 5. Places, pins and discovery

Three levels:

- **Major destinations** (map pins, Primary/Secondary): settlements, dungeons, boss territories, guild
  houses, waystones, realm exits.
- **Secondary places** (usually unpinned): ruins, towers, the broken bridge, standing stones, camps.
- **Micro-interest** (never pinned): cairns, a burnt waggon, a dead camp, a roadside stone.

**Discovery** (`MapDiscoveryRules`, `MapService`):

- *Region knowledge* (`RevealWithCell`): the capital's districts and the Crossway in the Ember Crown;
  the Clan Hold in Frostfang. Nothing else arrives with the region.
- *Seeing it*: a Primary place reveals within 190 m and a Secondary place within 80 m when the terrain
  gives a line of sight to its silhouette; services reveal at 20 m.
- *Being told*: taking a quest reveals every place its objectives name; `RevealFlagId` reveals a place
  when a story flag is set.
- Concealed places keep `RequiredFlagId` semantics (the Emberbound Undercroft).

**Map hierarchy.** Tier culling by zoom (`MapTiers`): settlements and major places at realm zoom,
waystones/dungeons/landmarks at regional zoom, shops and services at settlement zoom. The land layer is
the region's shaded relief from the bake (`MapCartography.Relief`), not per-cell rectangles.

**Fast travel.** Ember Crown: the Crown Square, the Crossway, Tarn's Landing, Hollowreach, Emberdeep
(the Embermarket's stone was retired: 138 m from the square's) and the player's holding. Frostfang: the
Clan Hold and Glacier Pass (the gateway to the aerie). Landings come from the bake (`WorldPlaceIndex`),
so a moved waystone moves every save's landing.

## 6. Authoring the world

1. **Macro geography and roads in world space** in the region spec: `geography()` (landforms and water)
   and `roads()` (polylines). `gen_regions.place_world` files each landform into the cell containing
   it; `gen_regions.realize_roads` splits each road at every cell edge and into ≤40 m graded pieces.
2. **The lattice last.** Rows and columns (`ROWS`, `COLUMNS`) are chosen after the places, irregular,
   ≤256 m a side. `tools/check_world_composition.py` fails places parked on cell centres, sharing an
   axis, evenly spaced, or roads crossing seams at edge midpoints.
3. **Settlement content at an origin.** `Cell.origin` is where a content frame stands in the world;
   `tools/shift_cell_content.py` moves the scene and its schedules to match.
4. **Street plans in world space.** `tools/district_layouts.py` → `tools/compose_district.py` writes
   structures into whichever cells contain them; the same plan becomes frontage terraces and pads in the
   spec. Then run `tools/gen_map_locations.py` (composition strips pins parented to generated nodes).
5. **New cells get scenes** from `tools/new_cell_scenes.py`.
6. **Preview before baking:** `godot --headless --path . -- --worldmap <dir> [--grid] [--scale m]`,
   and `--raw <region> x0,z0,x1,z1 [--set Key=Value,...]` to try the bare generator.
7. `python tools/world_bake.py --bake`, then the gates (`python tools/embervale.py world --mode fast`
   and `--mode engine`).
